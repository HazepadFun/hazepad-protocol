LAYER 01

THE TOOLKIT.

Five parts that move a trade from a swap on Solana to a payout in a holder's wallet.

Fee GateMODULE 01

Catches the creator cut of each swap as it settles, so nothing sits idle between trade and route.

Route MapMODULE 02

Your split lives here. Up to four routes, shares in whole steps, always adding to a full hundred.

Rig DeskMODULE 03

Turns routed funds into hash: rented capacity, machines you own, or spot power from open markets.

Payout LedgerMODULE 04

Records what the rigs return and posts the tally where anyone can check it.

Holder WeightsMODULE 05

Reads balances at each payout snapshot and divides the haul in proportion.
